Dillion:
Last Zone IN DEVELOPMENT

A battle royale set in the same world as Dilly Gone Mad, played offline against bots. Drop from a transport plane, loot up, fight 24 rival contractors and stay ahead of the shrinking gas. Last one standing gets paid. These are real captures from the first build, not mock-ups.

Title screen: the island, the LOS PEPINOS hillside sign, and Dilly as the playable operator.

What’s coming

  • 8 guns with recoil, aim-down-sights, reloads, armour plates and healing
  • ~24 bots that loot, fight and run from the gas
  • The shrinking gas circle with a full map
  • A Los Pepinos district with the pier and Ferris wheel
  • Later: squads, vehicles, and online multiplayer

Honest notes

  • Early build: the art is low-poly, not photoreal. Characters are a free-licence mannequin in kit.
  • Buildings are still simple boxes. Los Pepinos isn’t built yet.
  • Guns, loot, bots and gas are coded but not yet shown here.
  • It isn’t playable on the site yet. Check back for the first release.
