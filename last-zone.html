How to play

Ride the transport over Zone Nine and jump when you like (you're pushed out at the end of the route). Steer your free-fall, pop the chute, land near loot. Pick up guns, ammo, armour plates and medical kit. The safe zone shrinks in six stages; the gas outside hurts more each stage. Be the last contractor standing.
