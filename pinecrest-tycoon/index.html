🎄 Welcome to Pinecrest Tree Farm!

Run your own 2.5D isometric Christmas tree farm! · Created by King

  • All-Season Crops & Trees: Open Plants to grow Spring tulips, Summer strawberries, blueberries, sunflowers & apples, Autumn pumpkins, and Winter Christmas trees—or zone automated fields in Plots!
  • Festive Peak Season: In Nov–Jan, snow falls and families visit to pick mature trees, sip Hot Cocoa, warm up by Fire Pits, and visit Santa!
  • Checkout, Tractor & Boats: Guests pay at Saw & Checkout Sheds. Open Tractor (🚜) to run a hayride between stops, or Boats (🚣) to place a dock on any pond edge for guest rowboat rentals!
  • Expand, Sculpt & Bridges: Every new farm generates random hills, streams, & ponds! Use Buy Land (🚩) to expand, Terrain (⛰️) to raise/lower land or fill ponds with water, and Wooden Bridges from Paths & Decor to cross water.
  • Desktop Controls: Drag with Right-Click or Middle-Click (or Arrow/WASD keys) to pan • Mouse wheel to zoom • Q / E to rotate camera 90° • Click any tree, stall, guest, or staff to inspect.
  • Touch Controls: Drag with 1 finger to pan • Pinch with 2 fingers to zoom & pan • Tap any tile, guest, or staff to inspect or place • Use the Pan / Paint toggle in the tool bar to drag-paint paths, trees, or plots.
