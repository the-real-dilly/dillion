# Dillion

"The daily landing": seven prompts a day, rare answers launch you farther from Earth. Static site (no build step).
Includes Dilly Goes Wild (`dilly-goes-wild.html`, a standalone top-down city game featured on the home page and Games hub — `#play` / `#trailer` in its URL skip the title card), a mini crossword, Orbit Scramble, Rover Rescue, Memory Match and Higher or Lower, a daily mission twist, and a starship that launches in the background. Open `index.html` or run `python3 -m http.server`. An original Mars-themed take on the daily-prompt game format, with a parody pixel Elon who pops out of a crater.
Dilly Gone Mad (`dilly-gone-mad.html`): a cinematic 3D open-world driving game in Three.js (vendored in `vendor/`), with slapstick cartoon weapons (fifteen of them, from a lacrosse stick and a squirt gun to pie launchers, banana peels, bubble guns, a confetti bazooka and the piss nuke; nobody gets hurt). The world is about 3.6 km across: downtown plus streamed districts (hills with the LOS PEPINOS sign, suburbs over Dill Bridge, the port, the Salt Flats desert through the Pickle Ridge tunnel) on a road graph with a freeway loop, a 24-minute day, weather with storms and random street events; `?debug=1` shows streaming stats. `#play` starts the game, `#trailer` plays the trailer.
