## Dilly Strike materials

Photo-scanned PBR texture sets and HDRI panoramas from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), all **CC0 1.0 (public domain)**. Downloaded via the Poly Haven API (`api.polyhaven.com/files/<id>`), re-encoded for the web: `color.jpg` (sRGB albedo, quality 80, ambient occlusion baked in at 50 %), `normal.jpg` (OpenGL normal map, quality 90, 4:4:4), `arm.jpg` (AO / roughness / metalness packed in R / G / B, half resolution, quality 82). 2048 px for the three largest surfaces, 1024 px for the rest; HDRIs are the original 1k `.hdr` files. Loaded by `dilly-strike/src/photomat.js`; the procedural textures in `src/textures.js` remain as the fallback.
